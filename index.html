About the Exchange

Collectors first. Always.

Team Rockets Exchange started the way most good collections do — one binder, one grail, one late-night eBay refresh at a time. What began as a personal hunt grew into a vault of premium Pokémon cards shared with collectors who care about centering, surface and provenance as much as we do.

We are not a warehouse. Every card is photographed from our own inventory, described honestly — flaws included — and packed the way we would want our own grails packed. No hype, no hidden surprises. Just good cards and a straight answer when you ask about condition.

  • Authenticity guaranteedEvery card verified in hand. Graded slabs ship in their original cases — never cracked, never resealed.
  • Carefully packedSleeve, top-loader or slab bag, team bag, rigid mailer, bubble wrap. Sealed product double-boxed.
  • Fast, insured shippingDispatched within 1–2 business days with tracking shared the moment it leaves the vault.
